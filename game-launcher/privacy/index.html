Privacy policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Game Launcher for Steam is a Stream Deck plugin made by QuinTools. It does not collect, store or share personal data.

What the plugin reads

The plugin reads files from your own Steam installation on your computer: your installed games, when you last played them, and your Steam collections (such as Favorites). This information stays on your computer and is only used to show your games on your Stream Deck.

Network requests

For games that are in your collections but not installed, the plugin asks the public Steam Store (store.steampowered.com) and the Steam image servers for the game's name and artwork. These requests contain only the game's Steam app ID. They do not contain your Steam account, your name or any other personal information.

Stored data

Game names and generated key images are cached on your computer in %LOCALAPPDATA%\quin.tools\GameLauncherForSteam. Your settings are stored by the Stream Deck app.

Analytics

The plugin does not use analytics, tracking or telemetry.

Deleting data

Uninstall the plugin in the Stream Deck app and delete the folder above. Nothing is stored anywhere else.
